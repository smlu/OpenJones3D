std3D UV grid texture test vectors
==================================

Sources:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/textures/uv_grid_directx.jpg
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/textures/uv_grid_opengl.jpg

The BMPs in this directory tree are conversions of the Three.js
uv_grid_directx.jpg and uv_grid_opengl.jpg example textures for std3D
texture-mapping tests. The DX9 and OpenGL BMPs are 512x512; the DX6 BMP is
256x256 for devices that report the original 256 texture limit.
